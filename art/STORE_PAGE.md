## Uploading them

1. Go to **create.roblox.com → Creations → Hollow Pines**.
2. Open **Places**, then the **lobby** (the start place), then **Configure**, then **Thumbnails** (or **Basic Info**, depending on your Creator Hub version).
3. **Icon:** upload `icon_512x512.png`.
4. **Thumbnails:** upload `thumbnail_1920x1080.png`. You can add more later, such as screenshots from inside the cabin.
5. Paste the description below into the **Description** box, then **Save**.

Roblox checks new images before they show up, which can take a few minutes.

## Name

Hollow Pines

## Description (paste this)

The truck died at the end of the path. The cabin's door is open. The game on the table is the only way out.

1 to 4 players. A co-op horror card game.

• Take turns at the Parlor table: take a card, play a card.
• Room cards build the cabin around you, one room at a time.
• Progress cards move the Candle towards 3:00 AM. Keep it moving: the Hollow Man is following it.
• Clue cards make signs appear in the house: a lullaby with no one playing it, wet footprints, an empty rocking chair that won't stop rocking. Find them, write them in the Journal, and work out which room is the one real way out.
• Events drag everyone from the table: the power dies, the doors lock, something knocks, something pale moves only when you look away, a record plays and you must freeze when it stops, and sometimes he walks the halls himself.
• Pick your night: Normal, Hard or Nightmare, on the truck you ride up in.
• At 3:00 AM every way out unseals. Only one of them leads out. Choose.

Read the notes they left. Don't sit in the rocking chair. Don't answer the knocking.
